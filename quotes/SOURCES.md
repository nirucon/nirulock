# Sources of the built-in quote collections

Every quote was checked word for word against the source text named here
(fetched 2026-11-06). Whitespace and line breaks of prose were reflowed for the
screen; `…` marks an omission inside a passage.

## stoic-sv.txt (32 passages, the same as stoic-en)

Three passages are verbatim from a published public-domain translation:

- Seneca, *Om livets korthet. Om livslycka. Om själens ro. Valda brev*,
  tr. Johan Bergman, 2nd ed. 1918 (1st ed. 1912). Public domain (Bergman
  1864–1951). URL: https://runeberg.org/livkort/
  - Om livets korthet 1: https://runeberg.org/livkort/0023.html – 0024.html
  - Om livets korthet 2: https://runeberg.org/livkort/0024.html
  - Om själens ro 7: https://runeberg.org/livkort/0102.html – 0103.html
    (Bergman gives only selections of this work; this one is in the part
    headed "Om självkännedom", Stewart's chapter VII.)
  Spelling as printed (1918), not modernised.

The other 29 are Swedish renderings made for NIRUX from Long's and Stewart's
English (the stoic-en passages with the same attribution), marked
"NIRUX-tolkning" in the file. Small choices: Long's "[wealth or prosperity]"
written without brackets; Epictetus' "slaves" (the household) as
"tjänstefolk"; Long's glosses in parentheses left out.

Searched and not used: Projekt Runeberg has no Swedish Marcus Aurelius or
Epictetus. Litteraturbanken has Marcus Aurelius in Christopher Manderström's
translation (1755, *Betraktelser öfwer sig sielf*), whose 18th-century Swedish
would need far more than light modernising for a lock screen.
